🌿 About PlantDocBot

An AI-powered plant disease detection system that helps farmers and gardeners identify and treat plant diseases quickly and accurately.

🎯 Our Mission

PlantDocBot aims to make plant disease diagnosis accessible to everyone, from small-scale farmers to home gardeners. By leveraging artificial intelligence and deep learning, we provide instant, accurate diagnoses and comprehensive treatment recommendations to help protect your plants and improve agricultural outcomes.

✨ Key Features

🤖
AI-Powered Detection
Uses MobileNetV2 CNN architecture trained on thousands of plant disease images for accurate identification.
💬
Natural Language Chat
DistilBERT-powered chatbot that understands symptom descriptions in plain English and provides instant diagnoses.
📸
Image Upload
Simply upload a photo of the affected plant leaf, and our AI will analyze it within seconds.
💊
Treatment Guides
Comprehensive treatment recommendations including symptoms, prevention methods, and step-by-step care instructions.
🌍
Multiple Crops
Supports 38 disease classes across various crops including tomatoes, apples, grapes, corn, potatoes, and more.
⚡
Real-time Analysis
Fast inference time ensures you get results instantly, helping you take action quickly to save your plants.

🚀 How It Works

1. Image Upload: Upload a clear photo of the affected plant leaf or use our chat interface to describe symptoms.

2. AI Analysis: Our MobileNetV2 CNN or DistilBERT NLP model processes the input and identifies the disease.

3. Get Results: Receive instant diagnosis with confidence scores, detailed symptoms, and treatment recommendations.

4. Take Action: Follow the provided treatment guidelines to protect your plants and prevent further spread.
